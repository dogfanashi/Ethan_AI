Ethan AI

Ethan AI is an AI-powered assistant designed specifically for school help and computer programming.

The goal of Ethan AI is to provide students and developers with a simple, helpful, and easy-to-use AI assistant for learning, solving problems, understanding concepts, and writing or debugging code.

Your school assistant. Your coding partner.
